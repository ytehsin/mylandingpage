AI in the loop, not human in the loop

Most “AI translation” has it backwards.

The usual model lets a machine write and a human tidy up. I work the other way round: I write, and AI helps me check.

Human in the loop · the usual way
  1. Machine translates
  2. Human fixes what they spot
  3. Machine’s choices stay in

Fast and cheap, but the Hindi still sounds translated, and errors the reviewer doesn’t notice reach your users.

  1. I translate every sentence
  2. My AI tools check terms, numbers, spelling
  3. I review and sign off
